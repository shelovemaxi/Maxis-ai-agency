How Signal Wire decides what to show

Source priority: three verification tiers are used: official data and filings first, then major financial reporting, then specialist coverage. Discovery leads sit outside those tiers and cannot become confirmed by themselves.

Relevance score: a 0–100 prioritisation measure combining market breadth, event magnitude, urgency, source priority, cross-source support, and a noise penalty. It is not a price forecast.

Verification: “confirmed” means a primary source supports the factual claim; “2+ source checked” means distinct sources agree on the underlying event. Market connections are analysis, not guaranteed causation.
