Transparent methodology

Items are fetched from free public RSS/Atom feeds and GDELT/USGS endpoints. Rule-based phrase weights identify potential market relevance; percentage moves add a capped boost. Duplicate titles are collapsed and distinct-source corroboration adds 4 points. Scores are High (60+), Medium (30–59), or Low (<30). This is a research aid, not financial advice.

No account, API key, X/Twitter scrape, or paid World Monitor API is used. Source links remain visible for verification.
